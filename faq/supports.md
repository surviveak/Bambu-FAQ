## Q008 — What is “Support for PLA”?

It is intended primarily as a support-interface material for PLA. Using it only at the interface usually saves material and reduces tool changes compared with building the entire support structure from it.

## Q075 — Should I wait before removing supports?

Let the printed part cool to room temperature before aggressive support removal, especially when the geometry is thin or easily deformed. Drying filament after a print does not make already-printed supports easier to remove.

## Q104 — How do I select the filament used for supports?

In Bambu Studio, open the **Process → Support** settings (Advanced/Expert options may be required). Choose the filament for the **support/raft base** and, separately, the **support/raft interface**.

A useful strategy for a PLA model is to print the bulk support in PLA and use a compatible poor-bonding material such as PETG only for the interface. Dedicated Support for PLA is likewise commonly most efficient as interface material rather than the whole support.
