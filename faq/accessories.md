## Q030 — Can the H2 Series cutting upgrade draw with pens/markers?

The cutting/drawing workflow depends on the installed tool and compatible holder dimensions. Use the current Bambu accessory documentation for supported implements and setup rather than assuming any marker will fit.

## Q031 — Can the laser and cutting upgrade coexist?

They are different tool workflows/accessories. Physical installation and changeover requirements should follow the current H2C accessory documentation.

## Q032 — 40 W laser

The H2C can be configured with the appropriate laser accessory/hardware package. Follow Bambu's safety, enclosure and installation requirements; do not improvise laser interlocks or ventilation.

## Q033 — Bird's Eye Camera vs Live View

These are distinct camera/view functions. Camera capabilities, availability and streaming features can depend on the installed H2C hardware and firmware.

## Q074 — Is the Vision Encoder a separate accessory?

The Vision Encoder workflow uses its dedicated calibration hardware/pattern. It is used for precision calibration rather than normal printing.

## Q079 — Useful nozzle/hotend mix

A practical collection can include 0.2 mm for fine detail, 0.4 mm standard for general work, 0.4 mm high-flow for speed, and 0.6 mm wear-resistant for abrasive engineering filaments. Filled CF/GF materials require abrasion-resistant hardware compatible with the filament and printer.
